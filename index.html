1. Upload Website / App Reward +$0.30 / Upload
Every time you submit a brand-new, unique website or web application in Submit Application, you immediately earn $0.30 added directly to your OSSIAN PRO Balance. Remember: duplicate websites already uploaded by the company or any user are automatically blocked and cannot be re-submitted.
2. User Favorite Reward +$0.80 / Favorite
Whenever users tap the Heart icon to add your uploaded website to their Favorite Apps, you earn $0.80 per unique user favorite. High-quality and useful websites attract more favorites and grow your dollar balance faster.
3. Popular Top 3 Daily Bonus +$2.50 / Day
When other users (excluding the uploader) open and use your uploaded website enough to push it into the Popular Top 3 (#1, #2, or #3), you earn $2.50 every day for as long as your website remains inside the Top 3 Popular rankings.
4. Paid Websites & Token Deduction Token Gate
Websites or apps marked as Paid require active Tokens in your account. Any tokens used on a paid app/website are deducted immediately from your total tokens and the exact remaining token balance is displayed everywhere. If your balance reaches 0 Tokens (or fewer tokens than required), paid websites and apps cannot run.
5. OSSIAN TOK Privacy & Ownership Private Sealed QR
Both Login QR and OSSIAN TOK QR codes are cryptographically sealed so scanning them in Google or external scanners reveals zero personal user details. Your OSSIAN TOK is strictly locked to your UID and device—another user cannot use your tokens or Token QR in their account—and you can reset your Token QR anytime.

Privacy Policy

1. Data Collection & Storage

OSSIAN PRO respects your digital privacy and safeguards every piece of account information stored across our cloud infrastructure. During registration, we record your Name, Email, permanent UID, Invite Code, Balance, Token count, Device Identifier, Hardware Fingerprint, and Public IP Address strictly for authentication, fraud prevention, and multi-account enforcement.

2. Cryptographic & QR Privacy Protection

Your passwords and Banking Security PINs are protected using salted SHA-256 cryptographic hashing, while all Login QR and OSSIAN TOK QR codes are sealed into unreadable encrypted tokens so third-party scanners like Google cannot extract any personal details. OSSIAN TOK credentials are bound strictly to your own UID and device so other users cannot redeem or spend your tokens. Our anti-capture blackout shield blocks unauthorized screenshots and screen recording, and copied credentials clear from your clipboard after thirty seconds.
